We are always interested in hearing from motivated individuals who are passionate about neuromotor rehabilitation research. Our lab provides a collaborative, supportive environment for trainees at all levels.

## What We Look For

- Curiosity about the neural mechanisms of motor control and recovery
- Interest in translational research that improves the lives of people with neurological conditions
- Strong work ethic and ability to work both independently and as part of a team
- Backgrounds in neuroscience, rehabilitation science, kinesiology, biomedical engineering, or related fields are welcome

If you are interested in joining the lab, please send us a message through our [contact form](/#contact) with:

2. Your CV or resume

We review applications on a rolling basis and will respond to all inquiries.
